## I turn emerging tech into things you can actually use.

I'm **Hugo Chen**, an AI product engineer in Shenzhen. I work where product
interfaces meet model behavior: creative tools, coding agents, and systems for
learning difficult things in public.

Right now, I'm:

- shipping an image-first creative workbench with Canvas and WebGPU;
- building coding-agent runtimes from first principles;
- studying language models from the tokenizer up through Stanford CS336;
- turning long-form talks into searchable, timestamped knowledge.
